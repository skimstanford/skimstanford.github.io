ALEXANDER KIM is a tenor, conductor, and organist based in Southwest Germany. View upcoming engagements ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- ALEXANDER KIM grew up in the United States and studied church music at St. Olaf College in Northfield, Minnesota and at the Evangelische Hochschule für Kirchenmusik in Tübingen. Currently, he is studying Choral Conducting at the State University of Music and Performing Arts Stuttgart (HMDK Stuttgart) in the conducting class of Professor Denis Rouger. He is active as a soloist in concerts and has sung in choirs such as figure humaine kammerchor (Stuttgart, Germany) and the St. Olaf Choir (Northfield, MN, USA).
