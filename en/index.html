BIOGRAPHY

Alexander Kim grew up in the United States and studied church music at
St. Olaf College in Northfield, Minnesota, where he earned a Bachelor
of Music summa cum laude, and at the Evangelische Hochschule für
Kirchenmusik in Tübingen, where he completed the B-Examen. His teachers
include Denis Rouger, Dr. Anton Armstrong, Mihaly Zeke, Marius Mack
(choral conducting), Dr. Catherine Rodland, KMD Ingo Bredenbach (organ),
Wendy Hillhouse, and Guillermo Anzorena (voice). He served as cantor at
First English Lutheran Church in Faribault, Minnesota, before moving to
Germany. He later worked as a guest teacher at the Martin Luther Schule
in Rimbach, Hesse, and as cantor in Königsfeld in the Black Forest. Since
2026 he has worked as a tenor, voice teacher, and choral conductor in
Tübingen and Stuttgart. He is currently pursuing a part-time Master's
degree in Choral Conducting in the class of Denis Rouger at the HMDK Stuttgart.
